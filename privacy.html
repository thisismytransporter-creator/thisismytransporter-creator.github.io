Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Who uses this app

Print Control is used only by its owner, on their own computer, with their own Gmail account. It is not available to anyone else.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the app connects to the owner's Gmail inbox over IMAP and SMTP. It uses that access only to:

  • read new order notification emails from online marketplaces,
  • mark those emails as read once handled, and
  • send print status and alert emails to the owner's own address.

It does not delete email, and does not read, send or change anything else.

What it stores

All data stays on the owner's computer. The app keeps the order details needed to print (item and quantity) and, in its log, the subject and sender of order emails. Buyer names and addresses are not stored. The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on that computer. The app never receives the Google account password.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or sent to any third party. No data is used for advertising or to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the app's Settings page or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder removes everything it has stored.
